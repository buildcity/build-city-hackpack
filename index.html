Quick start

1 · Check in

Open the City Node app onboarding (citynode.app/onboard).

2 · Create a NEAR wallet

Use Meteor Wallet: open wallet, create a password, claim your .near name, create the wallet, save your seed phrase. The submission form asks for a mainnet NEAR payout account. Never share your seed phrase.

Watch: Wallet Creation

3 · Claim $10 in AI credits

Go to nearbuilders.org/dashboard: connect with NEAR, sign the message, publish a builder profile, claim $10.00, copy your key. One NEAR AI Cloud API key per builder; the offer closes 11/15/2026.

Your key starts with sk- and shows once. Never show it on screen, paste it into a chat or commit it.

Watch: Claim AI Credits

4 · Point your agent at NEAR

Fastest, OpenCode: install OpenCode, run connect, search "Near AI cloud", paste your key, pick a Confidential TEE model.

Watch: NEAR DevHub, 3 min

Any OpenAI-compatible client (Cursor, Continue, Cline, Aider, Zed, Goose, LiteLLM…):

Settings
base URL: https://cloud-api.near.ai/v1
model:    z-ai/glm-5.3-flash

Or try one call from a terminal (put your key in NEARAI_API_KEY first, never in the command):

First call
curl https://cloud-api.near.ai/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NEARAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "z-ai/glm-5.3-flash",
       "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Say hello in one sentence."}],
       "chat_template_kwargs": {"enable_thinking": false}}'

GLM 5.3 Flash is NEAR's own Confidential TEE model. Thinking is on by default, so answers can take a minute; the last line turns it off. Live model list: cloud-api.near.ai/v1/models (checked Nov 10).

Pick the right privacy tier

TierRuns wherePrivate?
Confidential TEENEAR's own GPUsYes. Prompt and output hidden from everyone, NEAR included
Attested 3PA third-party TEEEncrypted path; the provider publishes its own proof
IncognitoThe provider's serversNo. Only the gateway is in a TEE (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI and others)

Only the model call is private. Your laptop, your agent and the files it sends sit outside the TEE. Models and prices: near.ai/models

Section 4 · 11:30–12:00

Pick your idea

The idea worksheet is on the back of your agenda. Copy each prompt into your AI. 10 minutes with the AI, then 15 with people. Keep private details out of public chatbots.

1 · Brainstorm three ideas · 5 min with your AI

Prompt
I have 4 hours at a private AI hackathon, building on NEAR AI Cloud (models run in TEEs, so prompts stay private). Ask me 5 short questions, one at a time, about my skills and the sensitive-data problems I care about. Then suggest 3 ideas I can demo by 4:30, each fitting a direction (Healthcare & Personal Data, Finance & Legal, AI Agents, Agentic Commerce, Community & Nonprofits, Business Automation) or anything I'm excited about.

2 · Pick one and say it in one line · 5 min with your AI

Prompt
Take idea [A / B / C] and write it as one sentence of 20 words or fewer: "For [who], [project name] [does what], and it needs private AI because [why]." Give me 3 versions so I can pick one.

3 · Share it and get feedback · 10 min with people

Read your line to three people near you. Ask: is it clear? Would you use it? Do you want to build it with me?

4 · Sharpen the line and form your team · 5 min

Prompt
Here is my one line: [line]. Here is the feedback I got: [notes]. Rewrite the line so it is clearer, still 20 words or fewer.

Team of up to 4; solo welcome. Still looking? Post your final line in the Builders Cafe WhatsApp.

Use cases from the NEAR AI team

Security and investigations work that needs private AI. NEAR AI's attestation proves nobody, NEAR included, can see what you're testing.

Offensive security
  • Client pentests and red teaming
  • Pre-launch smart contract audits (about 1M tokens of context fits a whole codebase)
  • Bug bounty triage
  • Security product backends (OpenAI-compatible API, per-call attestation)
Defensive security
  • Incident response and forensics (no prompt or response retention)
  • Threat intelligence
  • Detection engineering
AI safety
  • Red-teaming AI systems
  • Training guard models
Regulated investigations
  • Fraud and anti-money-laundering (attestation as proof for compliance)
  • Legal and compliance review
  • Trust and safety moderation
Section 5 · 12:00–4:30

Build

Build with ICM

Interpretable Context Methodology: numbered folders are the steps, plain-text files hold the instructions, and one agent reads only what each step needs. You review between steps. It works with any agent that can read a folder: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Hermes, Cursor, OpenCode.

Start from your worksheet's final line: the starter guide has the prompt that turns it into a workspace.

ICM starter guide (PDF) icm-architect skill The paper

ICM was created by Jake Van Clief & David McDermott. Learn it free in the Clief Notes community on Skool.

Build with Hermes on NEAR AI Cloud

Install Hermes Agent, add your key, point its config at NEAR AI Cloud, add the icm-architect skill and start in your project folder: four moves, all on page 4 of the ICM starter guide.

Hermes setup (guide p. 4)

Build with OpenCode on NEAR AI Cloud

The quickest route: NEAR AI Cloud is built into OpenCode, and OpenCode reads ICM's AGENTS.md and Agent Skills on its own. Four moves:

  • Install OpenCode and open a terminal
  • Connect: run connect, search "Near AI cloud", paste your key, pick a Confidential TEE model such as z-ai/glm-5.3-flash
  • Add the skill:
Terminal
git clone https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect.git ~/.config/opencode/skills/icm-architect
  • Start in your project folder: open OpenCode in an empty folder and paste the workspace prompt from page 2 of the ICM starter guide, with your worksheet's final line in it
